Logo system — three marks, one direction

Recommended · 01

The Survey Node "A"

An "A" built from two survey lines and a level bar, crowned by a located node. Reads as the letter, an asset point, and a measurement at once. Scales to a 16px favicon.

Alt · 02

Waypoint + Verify

A located asset fused with a verification tick — the two halves of ASCURE: field capture and QA assurance. Warmer, more literal, very recognisable on a map pin.

Alt · 03

Survey Reticle

An instrument reticle locking onto a node — the most "control-room" of the three. Abstract and utility-agnostic, scales well beyond TNB to any asset class.

Heritage bridge — your asset-tag DNA, modernised

Bridge · recommended

Asset Tag + Node

Your origin story, literally: a physical asset tag (eyelet and all) carrying the survey-node "A". Honours where ASCURE began — tagging assets — in the new palette, and collapses cleanly to an app icon.

Bridge · 02

Framed Node

The old logo's open rounded-square frame, kept — but balanced and centred around the node "A" instead of floating off to one side. The most direct evolution of what you already have.

If you keep red

Heritage Red

Attached to the red — or want a Malaysian-identity nod? Keep it as the tag frame with a teal/cyan node inside. One caution: red also means "Critical" in-app, so we'd shift the shade to avoid a status clash.
